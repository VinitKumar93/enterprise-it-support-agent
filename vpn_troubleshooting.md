# VPN Troubleshooting
1. Verify that the device has working internet connectivity.
2. Confirm that you are using your own current VPN credentials. Never share or display passwords.
3. Restart the VPN client and try again.
4. Check whether the VPN service is available.
5. If the problem persists, create an IT support ticket and include the error message, time, and troubleshooting already attempted. Do not include passwords, Aadhaar numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
