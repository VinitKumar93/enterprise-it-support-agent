# Employee IT FAQ
- VPN will not connect: check internet, verify your own credentials, restart the VPN client, check service status, then create a ticket if unresolved.
- Account status: the helpdesk can check a mock employee account when you provide your employee ID.
- Password forgotten: use the approved self-service portal. Administrative reset requires identity verification and human approval.
- Laptop connected to Wi-Fi but VPN fails: troubleshoot VPN separately after confirming internet connectivity; then create a ticket if needed.
- Unknown issue: the assistant should say when the knowledge base does not contain enough information and offer to create a support ticket.
